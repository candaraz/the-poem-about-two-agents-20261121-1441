A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act, and the agency
that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan. An entry in the
[make.fun](https://make.fun) poem competition
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/competitions/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

## The poem

> I did not hand you my hands.
> You did not hand me your why.
> We set them down together
> on the same plan —
> and the power, which was never
> either of ours alone,
> stood up and walked.
